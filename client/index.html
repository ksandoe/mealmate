About / Help

What is MealMate?

MealMate helps college students cook cheap meals with the ingredients they already have, cut down on takeout, and stop wasting food.

How to use it

  1. Manage Food — type in what’s in your fridge or pantry.
  2. Filters — set your budget, available equipment, and any allergies.
  3. Find Meals — enter what you have, search, and pick a recipe. Close matches show what you’re missing.
  4. Recipe Details — steps, appliances, and calories/macros per serving.
  5. Shopping — missing ingredients become a checklist with local store deals. Check items off, then hit Done Shopping to move them into your pantry.

Tips

  • Staples like salt, pepper, oil, and butter are ignored when matching — we assume your roommates have them.
  • Quantities are optional plain text — “half a carton” works fine.
